OMENA AND UGALI
Serves: 4
Ingredients
For the omena:
- 250 g dried omena (silver cyprinid)
- 2 tablespoons cooking oil
- 1 onion, chopped
- 2 tomatoes, chopped
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 teaspoon grated ginger
- 1 cup water
- Salt to taste
- Optional: 1/2 cup coconut milk or chopped fresh coriander

For the ugali:
- 4 cups water
- 2 cups maize flour

Method
1. Place the omena in a bowl, rinse it well, and drain. If it has a strong smell, soak it in hot water for 5 minutes, then rinse and drain again.
2. Heat the oil in a sufuria or deep pan. Fry the omena for 3 to 5 minutes until lightly crisp. Remove and set aside.
3. In the same pan, fry the onion until soft. Add the garlic and ginger, and cook for 1 minute.
4. Add the tomatoes and cook until they soften into a thick sauce.
5. Return the omena to the pan. Add the water, season with salt, and simmer for 8 to 10 minutes. Stir in the coconut milk, if using, and simmer for 2 more minutes. Add coriander before serving, if desired.
6. To make the ugali, bring the water to a boil in a clean sufuria. Reduce the heat and gradually add the maize flour while stirring continuously to prevent lumps.
7. Keep stirring and folding the mixture until it becomes thick and pulls away from the sides of the pan, about 5 to 8 minutes.
8. Cover and cook on low heat for 3 to 5 minutes, then stir once more.
9. Shape the ugali onto a serving plate and serve hot with the omena and vegetables of your choice.
